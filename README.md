# What is Studly?

Studly is an AI-powered learning platform that transforms study materials and images of choice into bitesized educational episode with quizzes, flashcard and an xp and leaderboard system.

# Vision

Studly is built around one idea:

Learning shouldn’t feel like a chore.

Turn your notes into episodes.
Turn your friends into teachers.
Turn your group chat into a classroom.

Make studying hit different.
